# 00 · Concept

Version: v0.4. Day: 3. Status: in review.

What Lantern Market is, who it's for, and the few things that are settled. Turn structure goes in `01-turn.md`.

## Decided **DECIDED**

- Two players, one table. No online play in the first version. (day 1)
- A game lasts about 20 minutes: three nights of six rounds each. (day 1)
- Four card types: Goods, Stalls, Customers, Events. (day 2)
- No randomness in scoring. Luck lives in the draw only. (day 2)

## Proposals

- Hand size of 5, refilled at the start of each night **PROPOSED**. Keeps choices small for new players.
- A stall can hold at most 3 goods **PROPOSED**. Forces a choice between stocking and selling.
- Customers pay 1–4 coins **PROPOSED**. Example numbers; nothing has been played yet.
- The player with fewer coins goes first each night **PROPOSED**. A light catch-up rule.

## Questions for you

1. Should Events affect both players equally? **Default: yes.** If no, Events become attacks, and we need a defence card type.
2. Can goods be thrown away? **Default: no.** If yes, stalls never fill up and the 3-goods limit stops mattering.
3. Is there a hard end on night three, or a sudden-death round on a tie? **Default: hard end, tie goes to the player with more stalls.**

## Not in this version

- Online play, AI opponents, campaigns, more than two players.

## Choices I made for you

- Three nights, not four **PROPOSED**. Four nights ran past 20 minutes on paper.
- Coins are open information **PROPOSED**. Hidden coins invite counting arguments.
- No trading between players **PROPOSED**. It adds negotiation, which doubles game length.
